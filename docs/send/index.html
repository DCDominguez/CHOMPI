Sending replaces the sound CHOMPI is playing; nothing on its card changes. To keep it, save it on the panel (toggle down, hold CHOMPI, presets page, KEY_25, a slot, CHOMPI). Sounds are Choowi v7 instruments: make or convert them in Choowi's sound workshop. Volume low for the first try.
