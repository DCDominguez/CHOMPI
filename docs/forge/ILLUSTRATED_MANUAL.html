Choowi 0.19 · the first firmware of the forge, for CHOMPI

forge

Forge is a little workshop of custom firmware for CHOMPI. Choowi is the first thing out of it: a playable synth, a TAPE-compatible sampler and looper, harmony, an arpeggiator and bass, an event recorder, and a little magic, all inside stock TAPE's workflow. Design sounds on the computer, by hand or with an AI, and send them over USB.

Quick start

  1. Plug in headphones, switch CHOMPI on and turn SW6 (volume) up a little. CHOMPI wakes up singing: it loads the last preset you used and plays a little chime while a rainbow runs across the keys. The very first time, before any preset has been used, it starts as a clean pass-through.
  2. No sound yet? Hold SW1 + SW3 for a second: surprise me rolls a brand-new sound. Or record one (toggle up, hold CHOMPI through the red count-in), recall a preset (toggle down, hold CHOMPI, hold KEY_22 1 s, press a lit white key), or send one from the computer with Forge Bridge.
  3. Play the 25 keys. Turn the knobs; press and release a knob for its next page.
  4. Stop everything: hold SW4 and SW3 together for 1 second (panic).

Hi, I'm Choowi, the forge's blacksmith. Stuck for a sound? Hold SW1 + SW3 and I'll forge you a new one.

Knobs and knob pages

Paging and every knob's sound checked on CHOMPI

TAPE's pages come first, then Forge's synth controls. Press and release a knob to step to its next page; its light shows the page. Hold it for 1.5 s without turning to put that control back to the preset's value. One click moves 3 % on SW1–SW3, as on TAPE.

Synth patches. On sampler patches page 1 is TAPE's: pitch (SW4), sample start (SW1), sample end (SW2); SW1 page 2 is attack, SW2 page 2 release and page 4 loop crossfade. A patch can add its own control as each knob's last page.

One colour rule

Wherever you pick from a list or step through pages, the colours go in the same order: the 1st is always white, the 2nd green, and so on. Pressing a knob to its next page flashes that page's colour for a moment, then the light shows the value again.

Pitch, gain, saturation and the DJ filter are performance settings, as on TAPE: they stay when you change presets and reset at power-on.

TAPE's menu page

Checked on CHOMPI

White keys: chromatic, load and play that slot (KEY_15 is the recording); kit, pick a slot for save, copy or erase. Save, copy and erase finish with a press of CHOMPI.

Recording

Recording and playback checked on CHOMPI
  1. Toggle up, the record position. The CHOMPI light becomes the input meter and you hear the input in the headphones.
  2. Hold CHOMPI. CHOMPI and the white keys blink red three times (1.5 s count-in). Letting go now records nothing.
  3. Recording starts when the blinking stops (CHOMPI red). Release to stop. The keys play the take at once, chromatically and normalised.

Too quiet or distorted? Short-press SW6 and turn it for input gain (75 % at power-on), short-press again for volume. Up to about 87 s. The take stays until power-off; save it to a slot with KEY_25 on TAPE's page to keep it.

Harmony: one key, one chord

Chords and colours checked on CHOMPI

Each key plays a whole chord in the key you choose, through the instrument and to MIDI out. Synth patches and the chromatic sampler. Quick on / off: hold SW1 + SW2 for 1 s. Settings: TAPE's page, hold KEY_21 for 1 s (purple); here a key sets the tonic, which lights white, its scale blue.

The keys show the chords. With harmony on, every key glows in the colour of the chord it plays: orange major, blue minor, red diminished, pink augmented, teal sus4. Hold Shift and they change to show the shifted chords.

The keys tell you what they play: orange happy (major), blue moody (minor), red tense. Hold Shift and watch them change.

Static layout (the default) in C major, menu closed, coloured as CHOMPI lights them. The white keys C3–B4 are the chords I to vii twice; C5 is Shift. In Real layout the key you press is the chord's root.

Event recorder and projects

Record, loop, stop and clear checked on CHOMPI

Records what you play, keys, MIDI notes in and knob moves, on the clock, and loops it over 1–8 bars. It plays back like a second player: through harmony, the arp and the bass, and out of MIDI. It is separate from the audio looper. The controls are the parts page's F#4, G#4 and A#4.

Arm on the parts page, release CHOMPI and play; to close the loop just tap CHOMPI (toggle down). The same tap stops it and plays it again. Opening the menu and pressing F#4 still works. Up to 1,024 events; a take closes by itself after 8 bars. Saving a preset saves the loop beside it.

The sound

Virtual analog synth

Two oscillators (sine, triangle, saw, square; interval and detune), noise, a resonant low-pass per voice with its own envelope, an amp envelope with velocity, an LFO (sine, triangle, square, sample-and-hold) to pitch, filter or level, glide, and a voice count set by the patch.

Sampler

TAPE's sample files from the card (jammi_… chromatic, cubbi_… kit), up to seven voices, with each slot's own settings shared with TAPE in presets.json.

Patches

The knobs tweak a sound and the presets page saves it. Waveforms, oscillator 2's interval, LFO shape, voices and glide come from the patch: make it in Choowi's sound workshop on the computer, by hand or by describing it to any chatbot.

Magic

Wake-up, surprise me, mutate checked on CHOMPIChomp, dream, ripple: on CHOMPI, final check to come

Wakes up singing

Switch CHOMPI on and it comes back with the last preset you used, plays a little four-note chime through it, and runs a rainbow across the white keys.

Surprise me

Out of ideas? Hold SW1 + SW3 for a second: a brand-new sound, sometimes with chords in your key, sometimes with an arp, sometimes a gliding solo voice. Keep holding and it rolls another every 1.5 s; let go on one you like. Your volume, key and tempo stay put. The knob lights flash new colours each roll.

Chords and arp come and go, on purpose. Each roll decides them too: about 1 roll in 4 turns harmony on, about 1 in 5 starts the arp, and a roll that doesn't pick them switches them off, even if you had them on. Want chords on a rolled sound? Switch harmony on yourself (SW1 + SW2), then save. Mutate leaves them alone.

Mutate

Hold SW2 + SW3: the sound you have shifts a little, brighter or darker, longer or shorter, a different echo, so it drifts instead of starting over. Keep holding to drift further. The knob lights flash teal.

Harmony's secret modes

On the harmony page, turn SW3: green Chord Bloom up, yellow down, orange random (each chord grows one note at a time and stays), red Chord Conversations (let a chord go and, a bar later, Choowi answers with a little phrase). Harmony Mischief: hold a chord, then hold SW1 + SW3: the chord bends (sus, borrowed, inversion, a semitone up); let go of the knobs and it comes home. Not saved; off at power-on.

Chomp to instrument

Record anything, your voice, a door, a cup, then hold SW1 + SW3: the roll keeps your recording and builds an instrument around it, often with chords in your key and an arpeggio. The keys sparkle with every roll.

Dream mode

Leave CHOMPI alone for two minutes and it starts dreaming: a quiet, slow line in your key, its notes breathing purple on the keys. It needs the toggle down, the menu closed, a synth or chromatic sample sound (not a kit), and the looper and recorders idle. Any key, knob, SW5 or MIDI arriving stops it and restarts the two minutes (so a connected computer can keep it awake: try it unplugged). After three minutes it goes to sleep until you come back.

Like it? Save it to a preset slot. Neither gesture changes a knob page. When a loop closes, a green ripple runs out from the middle key.

SW6: battery and warnings

Safe mode

Safe mode means Forge crashed three times within 10 seconds of starting, so it started without the card's settings, presets and samples. Playing still works and the card is untouched. Copy FORGE/RESTARTS.TXT off the card, then switch off and on.

Power and battery

Battery reporting checked on CHOMPI

The power switch turns CHOMPI on and off; it charges either way. A USB-C wall charger is faster than a computer.

Battery level: hold SW6 for 2 s. White is charged, green good, yellow low.

Over USB: python host/forge_host.py battery prints the level, USB power, charging and whether a firmware install is allowed. Connect CHOMPI in the bridge shows it too.

Low battery: the knob and CHOMPI lights flash amber for 15 s, then CHOMPI shuts down. Plugging in during the flashing cancels it. Afterwards the switch alone won't wake it: plug in USB.

Storage mode (as stock): hold CHOMPI + PLAY + LOOP while switching on.

On a computer port with a low battery, CHOMPI can stop at once and stay dark. Use a USB-C charger: plug in, switch off, wait 5 s, switch on.

Installing and updating

SD-card install done on CHOMPI

First install: SD card

  1. Charge first: the SW6 battery light green or white.
  2. Back up the card. In its top folder rename every file whose name contains .bin, e.g. CHOMPI_TAPEv2_0.bin → CHOMPI_TAPEv2_0_bin.old.
  3. Copy FORGE.bin to the top folder.
  4. Card in, power on: rainbow lights while it installs, then Forge starts.

Updates: over USB

  1. Forge Bridge → Connect CHOMPI → Card & firmware → Install this kit's firmware.
  2. When the CHOMPI key blinks white, press it within 15 s.
  3. CHOMPI restarts and installs. Connect CHOMPI shows the new version and build, e.g. Forge 0.19.0 (build 7efb87e). If it stays all white after the install, switch it off and on.

Back to stock TAPE: rename CHOMPI_TAPEv2_0_bin.old back to .bin, remove FORGE.bin, power on.
