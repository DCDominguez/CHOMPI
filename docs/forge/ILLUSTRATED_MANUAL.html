Quick start

  1. Plug in headphones, switch CHOMPI on and turn SW6 (volume) up a little. Forge starts as a clean pass-through: line in, or the built-in mic when nothing is plugged in, goes to the output.
  2. Load a sound. The keys play nothing until you do. Record one (toggle up, hold CHOMPI through the red count-in), recall a preset (toggle down, hold CHOMPI, hold KEY_22 1 s, press a lit white key), or send one from the computer with Forge Bridge.
  3. Play the 25 keys. Turn the knobs; press and release a knob for its next page.
  4. Stop everything: hold SW4 and SW3 together for 1 second (panic).

Knobs and knob pages

Paging and every knob's sound checked on CHOMPI

TAPE's pages come first, then Forge's synth controls. Press and release a knob to step to its next page; its light shows the page. Hold it for 1.5 s without turning to put that control back to the preset's value. One click moves 3 % on SW1–SW3, as on TAPE.

Synth patches. On sampler patches page 1 is TAPE's: pitch (SW4), sample start (SW1), sample end (SW2); SW1 page 2 is attack, SW2 page 2 release and page 4 loop crossfade. A patch can add its own control as each knob's last page.
Pitch, gain, saturation and the DJ filter are performance settings, as on TAPE: they stay when you change presets and reset at power-on.

Recording

Recording and playback checked on CHOMPI
  1. Toggle up, the record position. The CHOMPI light becomes the input meter and you hear the input in the headphones.
  2. Hold CHOMPI. CHOMPI and the white keys blink red three times (1.5 s count-in). Letting go now records nothing.
  3. Recording starts when the blinking stops (CHOMPI red). Release to stop. The keys play the take at once, chromatically and normalised.

Too quiet or distorted? Short-press SW6 and turn it for input gain (75 % at power-on), short-press again for volume. Up to about 87 s. The take stays until power-off; save it to a slot with KEY_25 on TAPE's page to keep it.

Harmony: one key, one chord

Software-tested only

Each key plays a whole chord in the key you choose, through the instrument and to MIDI out. Synth patches and the chromatic sampler. Open: TAPE's page, hold KEY_21 for 1 s. On this page a key sets the tonic; the tonic lights white and its scale blue.

Static layout (the default) in C major, when playing with the menu closed. The white keys C3–B4 are the chords I to vii twice; C5 is Shift. In Real layout the key you press is the chord's root.

Event recorder and projects

Software-tested only

Records what you play, keys, MIDI notes in and knob moves, on the clock, and loops it over 1–8 bars. It plays back like a second player: through harmony, the arp and the bass, and out of MIDI. It is separate from the audio looper. The controls are the parts page's F#4, G#4 and A#4.

Arm on the parts page, release CHOMPI and play; to close the loop, open the menu and press F#4 again. Up to 1,024 events; a take closes by itself after 8 bars. Saving a preset saves the loop beside it.

The sound

Virtual analog synth

Two oscillators (sine, triangle, saw, square; interval and detune), noise, a resonant low-pass per voice with its own envelope, an amp envelope with velocity, an LFO (sine, triangle, square, sample-and-hold) to pitch, filter or level, glide, and a voice count set by the patch.

Sampler

TAPE's sample files from the card (jammi_… chromatic, cubbi_… kit), up to seven voices, with each slot's own settings shared with TAPE in presets.json.

Patches

The knobs tweak a sound and the presets page saves it. Waveforms, oscillator 2's interval, LFO shape, voices and glide come from the patch: make it in the sound designer on the computer, by hand or by describing it to the AI, and send it.

SW6: battery and warnings

Safe mode

Safe mode means Forge crashed three times within 10 seconds of starting, so it started without the card's settings, presets and samples. Playing still works and the card is untouched. Copy FORGE/RESTARTS.TXT off the card, then switch off and on.

Power and battery

Battery reporting checked on CHOMPI

The power switch turns CHOMPI on and off; it charges either way. A USB-C wall charger is faster than a computer.

Battery level: hold SW6 for 2 s. White is charged, green good, yellow low.

Over USB: python host/forge_host.py battery prints the level, USB power, charging and whether a firmware install is allowed. Connect CHOMPI in the bridge shows it too.

Low battery: the knob and CHOMPI lights flash amber for 15 s, then CHOMPI shuts down. Plugging in during the flashing cancels it. Afterwards the switch alone won't wake it: plug in USB.

Storage mode (as stock): hold CHOMPI + PLAY + LOOP while switching on.

On a computer port with a low battery, CHOMPI can stop at once and stay dark. Use a USB-C charger: plug in, switch off, wait 5 s, switch on.

Installing and updating

SD-card install done on CHOMPI

First install: SD card

  1. Charge first: the SW6 battery light green or white.
  2. Back up the card. In its top folder rename every file whose name contains .bin, e.g. CHOMPI_TAPEv2_0.bin → CHOMPI_TAPEv2_0_bin.old.
  3. Copy FORGE.bin to the top folder.
  4. Card in, power on: rainbow lights while it installs, then Forge starts.

Updates: over USB

  1. Forge Bridge → Connect CHOMPI → Card & firmware → Install this kit's firmware.
  2. When the CHOMPI key blinks white, press it within 15 s.
  3. CHOMPI restarts and installs. Connect CHOMPI shows the new version and build, e.g. Forge 0.15.2 (build c93357b).

Back to stock TAPE: rename CHOMPI_TAPEv2_0_bin.old back to .bin, remove FORGE.bin, power on.
