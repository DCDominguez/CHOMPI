# Glossary

The words the manual, the guide, the forge page and Choowivision use, arranged by page and function (Choowi
0.20). Some words mean different things in different places: those come first.

## Same name, different meaning

- **Key**: a **key** on the panel (C3 to C5, the black KEY_16 to KEY_25) — or the musical **key** your chords are in (C major, A minor), set on the harmony page.
- **Page**: a **menu page** (TAPE's, presets, harmony, arp · bass, synth, FX; the CHOMPI light's colour) — or a **knob page**, one of a knob's several jobs (press the knob to step).
- **Bank**: a **preset bank** (8, by colour: red to pink) — or a **sample bank** (5, by colour: purple, orange, teal, red, lime). Different lists.
- **Slot**: a **preset slot** (one of 15 white keys in a preset bank) — or a **sample slot** (a white key on TAPE's page; C5 is the recording).
- **Loop**: the **looper's** recorded audio — or TAPE's **auto-loop** of a held sample (SW1 on TAPE's page: off, loop, loop chords).
- **Speed**: SW4's first page, TAPE's **pitch** of the sound — or the **loop speed** (push and turn SW5) — or **Motion speed** on the FX page (hold SW3 and turn).
- **Record**: **recording a sample** (toggle up, hold CHOMPI) — or the **looper's take** (LOOP) — or the **event recorder** (F#4 on the arp · bass page), which records what you play, not audio.
- **Overdub**: adding a layer on the **looper** (LOOP while it plays) — or on the **event recorder** (G#4 on the arp · bass page).
- **Clear**: the **looper** (hold PLAY + LOOP 2 s) — or the **event recorder** (A#4 twice on the arp · bass page).
- **Filter**: the sound's own **filter** (SW5: darker or brighter) — or TAPE's **DJ filter** (SW3's third page: low-pass one way, high-pass the other).
- **Resonance**: the filter's ringing edge (SW4's third page) — or the **DJ filter's** resonance (on TAPE's page).
- **Tremolo**: on the synth page, **LFO → volume** (each voice wobbles) — or on the FX page, the **tremolo** effect (the whole mix pulses side to side).
- **Space**: SW3's first page, TAPE's reverb + delay — and the **Space** module on the FX page. The same control in two places.
- **Colour**: a **light's colour** (what the lights mean) — or the **Colour** module on the FX page (also SW3's second page).
- **Saturation**: the **Colour** module's first effect, warm drive (SW3's second page used to be called this).
- **Gate**: the **arp gate** (how long each arp note sounds) — or a sample's **hold** setting (sounds while held, or plays to the end).
- **Order**: the **FX order** (A#3 on the FX page) — or the arp's **as played** pattern (G3 on the arp · bass page).
- **Before / after**: the effects **before or after the looper** (whether the loop records them). Nothing to do with the compressor, which always comes last.

## Playing (toggle down, menu closed)

- **Toggle**: the switch by the CHOMPI key. **Down** is the menu position (you play; holding CHOMPI opens the menu). **Up** is the record position.
- **CHOMPI key**: hold it (toggle down) to open the menu; let go to close it; press it to confirm a save, copy or erase. A quick tap works the event recorder.
- **Knob page**: each of the four top knobs has several jobs; press and release a knob for its next one, hold it 1.5 s to put that control back. Its light shows the page.
- **Performance control**: a knob setting that belongs to CHOMPI, not the sound (pitch, gain, the DJ filter, as on TAPE): not saved in presets.
- **SW5**: the filter (turn); with a loop, push and turn for the loop's speed; click for normal speed.
- **SW6**: volume; short press for input gain; hold 2 s for the battery light.
- **Panic**: hold SW4 + SW3 for a second: every sound stops.
- **Surprise me**: hold SW1 + SW3: a brand new sound, sometimes with effects (keep holding for another).
- **Mutate**: hold SW2 + SW3: the sound drifts a little; keep holding to drift further.
- **Harmony on / off**: hold SW1 + SW2 (the knob lights flash green / red).
- **Dream**: hold SW4 + SW1: CHOMPI plays an ambient piece on its own, in your sound and key. A key stops it.
- **Nap**: after two quiet minutes unplugged, CHOMPI rests until you touch it.

## Record position (toggle up)

- **Recording a sample**: hold CHOMPI through the three red blinks (the **count-in**), then make your sound; let go to stop. The keys then play it.
- **Input meter**: the CHOMPI light while toggled up: how loud the input is.
- **Input gain**: how loud the microphone or line in comes in (short press SW6, then turn).

## TAPE's page (teal)

- **TAPE**: the stock CHOMPI firmware Choowi grew from; its controls are kept where they were.
- **Chromatic**: one sample played across the keys at different pitches (KEY_16; tap again for the next bank).
- **Kit**: one sample per white key, for drums (KEY_17).
- **Record source**: microphone, line in, or **resample** (record what CHOMPI itself plays).
- **Recording** (C5): your last recorded sample; it stays in memory until you switch off, unless you save it to a slot.

## Presets page (blue)

- **Sound** (or **patch**, **preset**): everything that makes CHOMPI sound the way it does, saved together (the effects too).
- **Recall**: hold CHOMPI, press a slot, let go. The last sound saved or recalled is the one CHOMPI wakes up with.
- **Save / copy / erase**: A#4 / G#4 / F#4, then the slot(s), then press CHOMPI.

## Harmony page (purple)

- **Harmony mode**: one key plays a whole chord, built from a musical key and mode.
- **Tonic**: the home note (any key on the harmony page sets it). **Mode**: the scale (major, minor, dorian…).
- **Chord size**: fifth, triad, 7th, 9th, 11th or 13th: how many notes each chord has.
- **Roman numerals**: a chord's place in the key: **I** is home, **V** the strongest pull back to it. Lower case (ii, vi) means minor; **°** diminished.
- **Secondary dominant** (V/ii, V/V…): a chord that pulls strongly towards another chord of the key.
- **Borrowed chord** (♭VII, iv, ♭VI…): a chord from the parallel key, for colour. **♭II** is the Neapolitan.
- **Static layout**: the white keys are the key's chords I to vii, twice. **Real layout**: the key you press is the chord's root.
- **Shift**: C5 in the static layout: hold it for the shifted chords.
- **Inversion**, **voice leading**, **open spread**: how a chord's notes are stacked; voice leading moves each chord as little as possible from the last.

## Arp · bass page (orange)

- **Arp** (arpeggiator): plays the held notes one at a time in a **pattern** (up, down, as played…) at a **rate** (1/8, 1/16…, t for triplets). **Gate**: how long each note sounds. **Latch**: keeps going after you let go.
- **Bass part**: plays the chord's root (or root and fifth) underneath, in its own octave.
- **Tempo** (BPM): beats per minute. **Tap tempo**: tap it in (C5). **MIDI clock**: another device setting CHOMPI's tempo.
- **Event recorder**: records what you play (notes, knob moves), not the audio; saved with a preset.

## Synth page (pink)

- **Synth**: CHOMPI's own oscillators making the sound. **Sampler**: playing sample files instead.
- **Oscillator**: the source of the tone; its **wave** (sine, triangle, saw, square) sets its colour. **Oscillator 2** adds a second one at an **interval** (a fifth, an octave…), slightly **detuned** for width.
- **Noise**: a hiss you can blend in.
- **Voices**: how many notes can sound at once. **Mono** is one, with **glide** sliding between notes.
- **Envelope**: how a note grows and fades: **attack** (how fast it starts), **decay** and **sustain** (where it settles while held), **release** (how long it rings after you let go).
- **LFO**: a slow wobble that moves pitch (**vibrato**), the filter, or the volume (**LFO → volume**). The **mod wheel** can control how much.

## FX page (green)

- **Module**: one of the four effect slots: **Space**, **Colour**, **Texture**, **Motion**. Each has three effects; one is chosen.
- **Space**: echoes and room. **Echo + room** (delay with reverb), **cloud** (a huge smeared wash), **tape echo** (repeats that wobble and darken).
- **Colour**: drive and grit. **Saturation** (warm), **fold** (glassy, metallic), **crush** (fewer bits, lo-fi).
- **Texture**: wear. **Cassette** (tape warble, hiss), **broken** (random dropouts), **squash** (heavy pumping compression).
- **Motion**: movement. **Chorus** (wide, lush), **phaser** (sweeping swirl), **tremolo** (the level pulses side to side).
- **Amount**: how much of a module you hear (its knob). All the way down, the module is **off**.
- **Character**: a second setting per module: hold its knob and turn.
- **Order**: the order the sound goes through the modules; each works on the one before's sound. **Classic** is Colour, Motion, Texture, Space. Tap A#3 to choose your own.
- **Side by side** (B3): normally the reverb comes after the delay, so every echo gets reverb too and the echoes blur into a wash. Side by side feeds the delay and the reverb separately: crisp, dry echoes with a room behind your sound. Good for plucks, drums and fast arps; leave it off for dreamy washes.

## Looper (PLAY and LOOP)

- **Looper**: records what you hear as audio and plays it round and round. **Take**: the first recording (LOOP to start, PLAY to end it).
- **Overdub**: a layer on top (LOOP while it plays). **Undo**: tap PLAY + LOOP together to take the last overdub back.
- **Pause / resume**: PLAY while it plays. **Clear**: hold PLAY + LOOP 2 s.
- **Overdub feedback**: in the menu, PLAY and LOOP set how much the old layers fade with each pass.

## The web pages

- **Forge page** (Choowi's forge): describe a sound, any chatbot writes it, hear it live on CHOMPI, save it.
- **Choowivision**: CHOMPI's panel on your phone or computer, live, with what every key and knob does on the page you're on (hover or tap for more).
- **Web MIDI**: how the browser talks to CHOMPI over USB (Chrome or Edge; Android phones, not iPhones).
- **OTG cable**: a USB-C cable or adapter that lets a phone talk to a USB device like CHOMPI.
- **v8 sound**: the 0.20 format, with the FX section. Older Choowi (0.19) can't load it.
- **Card**: CHOMPI's SD card: the firmware (FORGE.bin), samples and presets live there.
