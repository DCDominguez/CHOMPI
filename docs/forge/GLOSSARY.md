# Glossary

The words this manual, the guide, the forge page and Choowivision use, in plain terms. Choowi 0.20.

## Playing

- **Toggle**: the switch by the CHOMPI key. **Down** is the menu position: you play, and holding CHOMPI opens the menu. **Up** is the record position: holding CHOMPI records a sample.
- **CHOMPI key**: the big key. Hold it (toggle down) to open the menu; let go to close it; press it to confirm a save, copy or erase.
- **Menu**: what holding CHOMPI opens. The keys then change settings instead of playing; the CHOMPI light's colour tells you which page.
- **Page**: one screen of the menu: **TAPE's page** (teal), **presets** (blue), **harmony** (purple), **arp · bass** (orange), **synth** (pink), **FX** (green).
- **Knob page**: each of the four top knobs has several jobs; press and release a knob to step to its next one. Its light shows the page.
- **Performance control**: a knob setting that belongs to CHOMPI, not the sound (pitch, gain, the DJ filter, as on TAPE): not saved in presets.
- **Panic**: hold SW4 + SW3 for a second: every sound stops.
- **Surprise me**: hold SW1 + SW3: CHOMPI rolls a brand new sound (keep holding for another).
- **Mutate**: hold SW2 + SW3: the sound drifts a little; keep holding to drift further.
- **Dream**: hold SW4 + SW1: CHOMPI plays an ambient piece on its own, in your sound and key. A key stops it.
- **Nap**: after two quiet minutes unplugged, CHOMPI rests until you touch it.

## The sound

- **Sound** (or **patch**, **preset**): everything that makes CHOMPI sound the way it does, saved together.
- **Synth**: CHOMPI's own oscillators making the sound. **Sampler**: playing sample files instead.
- **Oscillator**: the source of a synth's tone; its **wave** (sine, triangle, saw, square) sets its colour. **Oscillator 2** adds a second one, at an **interval** (a fifth, an octave…) and slightly **detuned** for width.
- **Noise**: a hiss you can blend in.
- **Voices**: how many notes can sound at once. **Mono** is one, with **glide** sliding between notes.
- **Envelope**: how a note grows and fades: **attack** (how fast it starts), **decay** and **sustain** (where it settles while held), **release** (how long it rings after you let go).
- **Filter**, **cutoff**, **resonance**: the filter darkens the sound; SW5 sets where (cutoff), resonance adds a ringing edge there.
- **LFO**: a slow wobble that moves pitch (**vibrato**), the filter or the volume (**LFO → volume**).

## The FX page

- **Module**: one of the four effect slots: **Space**, **Colour**, **Texture**, **Motion**. Each has three effects; one is chosen.
- **Space**: echoes and room. **Echo + room** (delay with reverb), **cloud** (a huge smeared wash), **tape echo** (repeats that wobble and darken).
- **Colour**: drive and grit. **Saturation** (warm), **fold** (glassy, metallic), **crush** (fewer bits, lo-fi).
- **Texture**: wear. **Cassette** (tape warble, hiss), **broken** (random dropouts), **squash** (heavy pumping compression).
- **Motion**: movement. **Chorus** (wide, lush), **phaser** (sweeping swirl), **tremolo** (the level pulses side to side).
- **Amount**: how much of a module you hear (its knob). All the way down, the module is **off**.
- **Character**: a second setting per module: hold its knob and turn.
- **Order**: the order the sound goes through the modules; each works on the one before's sound. **Classic** is Colour, Motion, Texture, Space.
- **Side by side** (B3): normally the reverb comes after the delay, so every echo gets reverb too and the echoes blur into a wash. Side by side feeds the delay and the reverb separately: crisp, dry echoes with a room behind your sound. Good for plucks, drums and fast arps; leave it off for dreamy washes.
- **Before / after the looper**: whether the loop records the effects (before) or the effects play on the loop live (after).

## Harmony

- **Harmony mode**: one key plays a whole chord, built from a key and mode.
- **Key** and **tonic**: the home note your chords are built on. **Mode**: the scale (major, minor, dorian…).
- **Chord size**: fifth, triad, 7th, 9th, 11th or 13th: how many notes each chord has.
- **Roman numerals**: a chord's place in the key: **I** is home, **V** the strongest pull back to it. Lower case (ii, vi) means minor; **°** diminished.
- **Secondary dominant** (**V/ii**, **V/V**…): a chord that pulls strongly towards another chord of the key.
- **Borrowed chord** (**♭VII**, **iv**, **♭VI**…): a chord from the parallel key, for colour. **♭II** is the Neapolitan.
- **Static layout**: the white keys are the key's chords I to vii, twice. **Real layout**: the key you press is the chord's root.
- **Shift**: C5 in the static layout: hold it for the shifted chords.
- **Inversion**, **voice leading**, **open spread**: how a chord's notes are stacked; voice leading moves each chord as little as possible from the last.

## Arp, bass and tempo

- **Arp** (arpeggiator): plays the held notes one at a time in a **pattern** (up, down, as played…) at a **rate** (1/8, 1/16…, t for triplets). **Gate**: how long each note sounds. **Latch**: keeps going after you let go.
- **Bass part**: plays the chord's root (or root and fifth) underneath, in its own octave.
- **Tempo** (**BPM**): beats per minute. **Tap tempo**: tap it in. **MIDI clock**: another device setting CHOMPI's tempo.

## Looping and recording

- **Looper**: records what you hear as audio and plays it back round and round. **Take**: the first recording. **Overdub**: layering more on top.
- **Undo**: tap PLAY + LOOP together to take the last overdub back. **Clear**: hold them 2 s.
- **Loop speed**: push and turn SW5 while a loop plays; below zero plays it backwards.
- **Event recorder**: records what you *play* (notes, knob moves), not the audio; saved with a preset.
- **Recording a sample**: toggle up, hold CHOMPI through the red count-in. **Resample**: record what CHOMPI itself plays.
- **Input gain**: how loud the microphone or line in comes in (short press SW6).

## Presets and samples

- **Preset slot**, **bank**: presets live in 8 banks (by colour) of 15 slots (the white keys).
- **Sample bank**: the card's sample files, in banks named by colour (Choowi's own sounds are bank **purple**).
- **Chromatic**: one sample played across the keys at different pitches. **Kit**: one sample per white key (drums).
- **TAPE**: the stock CHOMPI firmware Choowi grew from; its controls are kept where they were.
- **Card**: CHOMPI's SD card: firmware (**FORGE.bin**), samples and presets live there.

## The web pages

- **Forge page** (Choowi's forge): describe a sound, any chatbot writes it, hear it live on CHOMPI, save it.
- **Choowivision**: CHOMPI's panel on your phone or computer, live, with what every key and knob does on the page you're on.
- **Web MIDI**: how the browser talks to CHOMPI over USB (Chrome or Edge; Android phones, not iPhones).
- **OTG cable**: a USB-C cable or adapter that lets a phone talk to a USB device like CHOMPI.
- **v8 sound**: the 0.20 format, with the FX section. Older Choowi (0.19) can't load it.
