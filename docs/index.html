meet Choowi

it wakes up singingswitch it on and your last sound comes back with a little chime and a rainbow on the keys.
one key, a whole chordharmony plays chords in your key, and the keys light up so you can see what's happy and what's moody.
out of ideas?hold two knobs and Choowi forges a new sound. hold two others and it drifts. record anything, roll, and it's an instrument.
it dreamsleave it alone for a couple of minutes and it plays quietly in your key, the keys breathing purple.
build sounds on the panelthe synth page: waves, LFO, voices and glide, no computer needed. or describe a sound to any chatbot in Choowi's sound workshop.
TAPE is still in therethe sampler, recording and looper work the way your hands already know.

put it on your CHOMPI

  1. download choowi-0.20.0-rc3-card.zip (release candidate): the firmware, Choowi's sounds and the manual.
  2. charge CHOMPI (battery light green or white: hold SW6 for 2 s).
  3. back up the SD card, then delete its sample files (jammi_*, cubbi_*) and every file with .bin in the name.
  4. copy everything from the zip to the card, card in, switch on. rainbow, then Choowi says hi.

want stock TAPE back? put your backup back on the card. the zip's NOTICES.txt has the licences.
