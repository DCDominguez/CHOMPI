# Choowi's sample pack

Two banks for CHOMPI's SD card, in TAPE's layout (Forge and TAPE both play them).
Copy the `.wav` files to the card's top folder. On CHOMPI: TAPE's menu page, **KEY_16** until
chromatic bank **d**, or **KEY_17** until kit bank **d**. Chromatic sounds play at their own pitch
on middle C. Banks a-c (TAPE's own samples) are untouched.
