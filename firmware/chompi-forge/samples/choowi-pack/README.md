# Choowi's sample pack

Forge's default samples: chromatic and kit bank **purple** (bank a) on CHOMPI's SD card, in
TAPE's layout (Forge and TAPE both play them). Copy the `.wav` files to the card's top folder (they replace
files of the same name). On CHOMPI: toggle down, hold CHOMPI, TAPE's page (teal light); press **KEY_16**
until its light is purple for the chromatic bank, then a white key picks a sound; **KEY_17** for the kit.
Chromatic sounds play at their own pitch on middle C.
