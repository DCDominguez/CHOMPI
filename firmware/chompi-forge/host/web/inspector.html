USB · NO CARD SWAPPING

Card & firmware

Firmware 0.7 or newer. Copies samples onto CHOMPI's SD card and installs firmware over this USB cable, so the card can stay in CHOMPI. Accepted: TAPE sample names (jammi_/cubbi_ + bank a–e + slot 1–14, .wav) and FORGE.bin. Each file replaces the one on the card only after it arrived complete and checked.

Connect to see the card folder.

Writes 12 starter sounds (pads, basses, keys, echoes, the TAPE kit) into slots 1–12 of the chosen bank on CHOMPI's card. Slots that already hold a preset are kept, never overwritten. Recall them on CHOMPI: toggle down, hold CHOMPI, press the white key.

Press the CHOMPI key on the panel now.

Its light blinks white for 15 seconds. CHOMPI then restarts; the bootloader installs the new firmware (rainbow lights) and Forge starts. Then press Connect CHOMPI again.

HANDS · EYES

Panel walk

About 10 minutes, once per unit. The bridge asks you to press, turn and flip every physical control and confirms each one itself from CHOMPI's switch readings; anything that arrives on a different control is recorded. Then it lights known patterns and asks which colour you see. Use the CHOMPI Panel Map if the keys are unlabelled. Nothing is written to the SD card; the walk leaves CHOMPI on the dry patch.
